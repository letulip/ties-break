# UI – 26 September 2026 review

## Verdict

The UI layer closed almost everything the 05.09 review asked of it: eleven of U-01…U-12 are fixed, each
with a mounted net that names the finding, and the three largest screens measured cheap to render
(1.0–2.6 ms main-thread per snapshot, baseline §C.2). This pass applied the parity convention to every
screen and composable instead of the three sites the parity wave guarded. Most of the UI already asks
the engine's own primitive (form A), but the sweep found one **live** restatement that is wrong: the
Season header counts the pro allowance "this season" and says it resets "when the season turns", while
the engine has counted birthday to birthday since 16.08. Three things matter most:

1. **E-01 (P1).** The Season header's pro allowance states a window the engine abandoned on 16.08. On
   the committed v46 career it reads "6 of 12" for 24 weeks of a season in which she has entered
   nothing, and no test reads the line.
2. **The UI has its own two-spellings class, even where no engine verdict exists.** Three plan-preset
   rows give two answers to "which preset is hers" (E-02). A second body of the engine's
   `eventIsHers` (E-06) and three copies of `isOfferLive` (E-07) agree today only because they are
   copies. Two predicates key behaviour off owner-owned display words (E-05), so a wording change
   the owner is entitled to make would silently turn off the silver trophy flight.
3. **Accessibility has one real gap and several remainders.** Home's rank chip has a static
   aria-label, so a screen reader never hears her rank (E-03). Three popups sit outside the
   focus-managed set, and one of them was lengthened after the popup law without the 375x667 net
   (E-08). The store's refusal line has one owner that five templates never adopted and that the
   blocking popups cannot show (E-09).

### E-01 · The Season header counts the pro allowance by season; the engine has counted birthday to birthday since 16.08
- Severity: P1
- Category: correctness-risk
- Evidence:
  - `src/components/screens/SeasonScreen.vue:782-786` renders
    `` `Pro entries this season: ${cap.used} of ${cap.limit}` `` from `snapshot.proEntryCap`.
  - `:1386` gives the line the title "…how many professional (W) events she may enter this season. A
    fresh allowance arrives when the season turns…".
  - The engine's figure is `proEntryCapUsage(world, world.week)` (`src/engine/world/snapshot.ts:2127`),
    which counts `proEntryWeeks` whose `kidAgeAt` equals her age now, a birthday-to-birthday window
    (`src/engine/world/entryCaps.ts:285-296`, "`entryCapUsage`'s birthday window verbatim").
  - The same screen's event-card pills say "counted from birthday to birthday" (`SeasonScreen.vue:1830`,
    `:1839`). The chip (`composables/tierState.ts:1018`, `:1033`) and the engine's refusals
    (`world/medical.ts:810-812`, `:828-830`) all say "next birthday". The engine's own note records the
    same fix on its side: "⚠ 'ON HER NEXT BIRTHDAY' AND NO LONGER 'NEXT SEASON' … the old sentence named
    the wrong date – and a refusal that names the wrong date is worse than one that names none"
    (`medical.ts:807-809`).
  - History: the header line and its title were written in `fdce8055` (02.08,
    `git blame -L 782,786`). The engine moved to the birthday window in `39b12224` (16.08, "the allowance
    is counted birthday-to-birthday") and the card pills followed 20 minutes later in `53223b3d`. The
    header was never re-aimed.
  - Measured with `npx vite-node …/probes/ui-pro-allowance-window.ts` (RAW/pro-allowance.log). The header
    renders on 44 of the 90 golden saves. On `v46`–`v49` (a real sixteen-year-old at week 155) it reads
    "Pro entries this season: 6 of 12" against **5 entries this season and 6 this age-year**. At the
    season turn (w156) the engine's count stays **6 → 6**, and it falls to 0 only at her birthday (w180).
    So for 24 weeks the header says she has used 6 of this season's 12 when she has entered nothing this
    season.
- Why it matters: this is brief §11's calibration class exactly – a screen restating an engine verdict
  and making a claim the engine never made. It sits on the one screen where entries are planned, so a
  parent budgeting W entries is told the wrong window. `git grep -lE
  "proBudgetLine|season-pro-budget|Pro entries this season" -- tests/ | wc -l` → 0: nothing reads the
  line, and CLAUDE.md invariant 4 notes a wording drift is the one diff no test catches.
- Proposal: the number is already the engine's; only the label and title restate the window. Which words
  replace them is **the owner's** (a question, below). Two forms exist on the same screen: the card pills'
  "counted from birthday to birthday", or the engine's refusal sentence. Once he rules, add a mounted
  case (form B) that poses `v46` and asserts that the header's window word and the pills' agree. Arm A:
  change `proEntryCapUsage`'s window and both surfaces must redden. Arm B: hand-code "season" on the header
  and only this case reddens.
- Blast radius: wording (invariant 4) – the fix is a copy change and waits for his ruling. No RNG keys,
  save schema or balance. Tests that would move: 0 on the line itself; `git grep -l "SeasonScreen" --
  tests/ | wc -l` → 45 files mount or pin the screen (none asserts this text).
- Effort: S
- Confidence: high. The engine window is in code, the probe reads the engine's own function on committed
  real careers, and the same-day commit history shows the divergence. A mounted render of `v46` would
  only restate it.
- Versus 05.09: new
- Verification: CONFIRMED – the header line and its title still state a per-season window with a reset "when the season turns" (fdce8055), while since 39b12224 (16.08) the number is `proEntryCapUsage`, counted birthday to birthday, as the same screen's pills, the engine refusal and decisions.md all say; one correction: on v46 the 6th entry is a w156 booking, so from w156 to w179 the header reads 6 against 1 entry this season, not 0.
