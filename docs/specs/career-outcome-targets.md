# Design targets — career outcomes (agreed with the owner, 26.07.2026)

The numbers the economy/progression waves aim at. Recorded here so the bench can be gated against
them instead of tuned by feel. **Not yet wired as bench assertions** — that lands with the economy
wave, once prize money and the earned valves exist to move them.

## The base matters: everything below is CONDITIONAL on the run reaching the horizon
"Reaching the horizon" = the family did not go bankrupt and she did not quit. A career cut short by
bankruptcy never got its shot at the pro contour, so measuring it against a tennis target would
count the same failure twice. Splitting the bases keeps the MONEY filter and the TENNIS filter
separable.

The bench must report every reach figure in BOTH bases (conditional, and of all starts) — the two
were confused once already in discussion, and the multiplication is where the design lives.

## The ladder

| Outcome | Target (of runs reaching the horizon) | ≈ of all starts (at 70% survival) |
|---|---|---|
| Family did not go bankrupt, 14→18 | **60-80%** (of all starts) | — |
| Saw the pro contour ($15k-analog events) | **50-65%** | ~35-45% |
| Lives from tennis (~top-250 analog) | **15-25%** | ~10-18% |
| Top-100 analog — a real star | **3-6%** | ~2-4% |
| Slam-level | <1% | <1% |
| She quit of her own accord | **5-10%**, reversible | — |

## Why these, and not the honest real-world numbers
Reality is harsher: ~70% of children leave organised sport by 13, and of juniors identified as
talented at 12-13 only a small minority remain on a high-performance path at 18. Copying that would
be accurate and bad: a 14→18 career is four seasons of real play, so a 10% success rate means nine
players in ten never see the content built for the pro contour at all. That is not honest
difficulty, it is hidden content.

So the honesty is placed where it costs the player nothing to experience: **most invested careers
should see the door; few should walk through it; almost none should become a star.** Bankruptcy is a
frustrating end (the run stops and the investment evaporates), so it stays the minority outcome;
falling short of the dream is a STORY, so that is where the attrition lives.

**Do not set survival and pro-reach both high.** They multiply: 70% × 70% ≈ 49% of all starts, which
means nearly everyone who stays solvent also reaches pro — the tennis stops filtering and money
becomes the only obstacle. The 65% ceiling on pro-reach exists to keep the climb meaningful.

## "She quit" — the conditions matter more than the rate
Owner-approved as a mechanic, but bounded:
- **Foreseeable**: a slow trajectory with signals (she withdraws, the relationship slides, her lines
  change, the coach warns), never a dice roll. Quitting after months of ignored warnings is fair;
  quitting out of nowhere is punishment.
- **Reversible**: prefer a BREAK — a season out, returning later and weaker — over a hard game-over,
  so it becomes a story branch rather than a deleted save. A rare permanent exit can stay, with an
  epilogue about who she became.
- Rate 5-10%, and only for players who pushed through every signal.
This needs the morale system (Phase 6); the personal-life arc uses the same parent-as-observer rule.
