# The child – wave 9 (W5), predicted before measured

The last step of the pregnancy branch and the layer's step 7½. The brief is
[life-wave-9-builder-2026-09.md](../plans/life-wave-9-builder-2026-09.md); every ruling quoted there
is his, 21.09. This file is invariant 5's half: what each constant was drafted at, what it was
predicted to do, and what the bench measured it doing.

⭐ **His framing is the wave's size**: «воспитания нет, всё верно, это просто ручка для династии
дальше». The child is the handle step 9 turns – enough presence that the career remembers her, and
not one mechanic more.

⚠ **This wave moves the parent's money zero times.** The wedding had no price, the birth had no fee,
and the child has no standing line – his ruling of 21.09, «думаю, что нет, это её ребенок», on the
seam round 23 #18 already built. It is the third money question in this branch and the third «no».

⚠ **And it owes NO schema move.** The brief drafted one appended field for T5; the shipped shape is
derived, so there is no migration, no golden fixture and no peel rung in this wave.

## 3. The shapes that were rejected, and why

* **A composure BUMP instead of room.** Refused on a measurement, not a preference: wave 8b's arm 7
  found a coached career reaching 96–98% of her own headroom by about twenty-two
  ([the-unclosable-head-2026-09.md](../research/the-unclosable-head-2026-09.md) §7), and the first
  child arrives at 24+. A bonus clamped to her rolled ceiling is worth almost nothing to almost
  everybody.
* **A persisted «bonus applied» field.** A derivation off `world.children` cannot be applied twice,
  and a field would be a second home for a fact the array already holds.
* **A weekly cost line.** His ruling; see the head of this file.
* **A cap on the number of children.** His ruling – «пусть решает арифметика» – so
  `repeatCountFactor` is the only thing making a large family rare.
