# Spec-let — Ladder-up: the junior international tier (owner data 25.07)

**Slice order:** after Season Planner. Pairs with the cohort pre-history investigation (high-tier
entrant selection needs meaningful rankings). Fictional analogues in-game — ITF is a trademark.

## The real ladder (owner's data, recorded verbatim in substance)
From age 13: the world junior tour — ~980 tournaments across 130 countries, the direct trampoline
into adult tennis. Six prestige levels by ranking points:
- **J500** (top): junior Grand Slams + the season Finals
- **J300 · J200 · J100 · J60 · J30** (entry level)
- **J30 + J60 = 75% of ALL tour events** — the bread and butter of beginners.

## Game mapping (design intent)
- Our locked `itf` tier becomes a FAMILY of J-levels (data-driven in TIERS — each level its own
  entry fee / travel band (international × wealthCorridor) / draw / points / everyNWeeks).
  Start with 2-3 levels (J30/J60 dense + a rare J300-analog), extend later; J500-analog waits for
  the prestige/finals content.
- **Overlapping point bands between J-levels** (owner: "календарь будет меняться в зависимости от
  нахлеста тиров J друг на друга") — as one level closes behind her, the next is already open, so
  THERE IS ALWAYS somewhere to go, (theoretically) every week. The J30/J60 density (75%) is what
  guarantees it — mirror that density in our calendar.
- Age gate 13+ (our start is 14 — open immediately); NO prize money at J-levels (real: juniors
  pay to play — the "invest without knowing the return" thesis at full strength: international
  travel $900-2000 × corridor, zero return until the pro-money tier).
- The abundance is the point (owner): with J-tiers + practices + vacations, empty weeks stop
  being boredom and become CHOICE — where to go, what it costs, what her body can take. The
  season planner's calendar UI must scale to this density (grouping/filters by tier).
- National H2 densification rides along (the pre-J bridge).

## Interlocks
- Cohort pre-history FIRST or together: J-level entrant fields must be ranking-meaningful.
- Bench re-runs mandatory: entries/season, travel burn (international corridors bite), condition
  math under denser calendars, injury panel vs anchors.
- The pro-money tier ($15k-analog, 15-16+, REAL prize income) stays a separate later milestone.
