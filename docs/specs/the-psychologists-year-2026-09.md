# The psychologist's year – reconciling his three rulings into one seat

Step 5 of the private-life layer accumulated THREE descriptions in three weeks, each his, none
withdrawn. This spec reconciles them so the wave builds one thing. The build plan's §6a is the
preserved 23.08 record and now feeds this document; the words, verbatim:

> **22.08, the shape:** «Б — массажист ездит, психолог работает дистанционно и стоит только
> зарплату. …да, надо распланировать.»

> **23.08, the levels:** «надо еще отдельным слоем добавить психолога туда как раз и его влияние,
> варианты работы с ним, по идее это 1 сессия в неделю но может быть тогда будут разные уровни
> психологов с разной эффективностью, надо подумать этот момент»

> **08.09, the year-focus (round 39 #7 tail):** «…можно в беклог отношений записать, что-то вроде
> возможности работать с психологом в плане хладнокровия (тот же Федерер вроде бы в какой-то
> момент осознал, что психует много и поработал над собой, стал лучше), как раз можно будет у
> психолога делать выбор над чем работать в ближайший год на выбор игроку.»

And a fourth job arrived 09.09 without being asked for: [who-she-is](who-she-is-2026-09.md) §2a's
drift needs «работа над собой» as the deliberate condition of the care-pole walk – a focus is the
natural home.

## 1. ⭐ The reconciliation in one sentence

**The YEAR-FOCUS is the choice of channel; the RUNG is how well the chosen work goes.**

The 23.08 design had one channel (the recovery slope) and made the rungs differ on it – and its
own arithmetic showed the top rungs blurring (§6a's honest problem: +3 vs +4 per week are
near-indistinguishable on weeks-under-the-knee). The 08.09 ruling dissolves that problem: the
rungs no longer have to be three speeds of one thing, because the player chooses WHAT the year is
for, and the rung scales THAT. «Разной эффективностью» reads as «how good the specialist is at
the work you chose», which is how hiring actually works.

⚠ **Focus effects never stack** – one focus per year is the whole point («выбор над чем работать

focus choice is JOINT – she can decline** (reads `bond`; his July arc «в зрелости рычаг – влияние

work** (who-she-is §2a – this seat never paywalls the road back from harmful parenting) and
**identity is immutable** – no focus changes WHO she is, only coping, communication, walls and
how she carries being known. The time-cost question is answered for v1: sessions cost money only
(the masseur's precedent – no calendar hours); the cool-head + training stack is bench-watched
against the training-only arm.

## 3. The rungs – who comes to the call

From §6a, kept: three rungs, prices in the game's own scale, weekly retainer, and **each rung
measurably better than the one below at THE CHOSEN FOCUS or it is re-priced** (the masseur's §4
law, now applied per focus):

| rung | price idea | reads as |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| a counsellor | ~$100/wk | below the masseur's entry – a weekly hour, not a specialist |
| a sport psychologist | ~$200/wk | between the masseur's entry and default rungs – the DEFAULT |
| a tour-grade specialist | ~$400/wk | the high coach's neighbourhood |

The 23.08 slope-blur problem does not return: rung separation is proven per focus on that focus's
own metric, and the completeness of the grid (4 focuses × 3 rungs × paired arms) is the wave's
bench, not a taste.

* **The hired-before-any-need corridor** (§6a question 4, kept): hiring before any shock or

  focus never fired, because a retainer that never works is the academy-fares failure (round 23
  #16) in a new coat. The focus model shrinks this risk by construction: cool head and the drift
  focus do slow work every held week, so «never fired» is mostly the recovery focus's exposure.

## 5. Temperament interplay – priced, not new machinery

Who-she-is gives every focus a natural clientele: an `intense` girl's shocks are deeper, so
recovery buys more weeks back; a `private` girl's wants are harder to read unaided, so
understanding-her is worth more exactly for her; the drift focus only means anything where a
leaning has room to move. The benches carry per-temperament arms and print these gradients – they
are expected, and they make the hire read as a decision about THIS daughter rather than a stat
subscription.
