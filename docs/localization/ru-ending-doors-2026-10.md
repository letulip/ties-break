# RU-12C – The nine ending doors and her leaving voice

Sources: `src/engine/ending.ts`, `src/engine/world/endings.ts`. Runtime currently has **nine**
`CareerEndingType` values: `stopped`, `college`, `bankruptcy`, `injury`, `natural`, `plateau`,
`peak`, `fall`, `family`. The canonical product context pack still says six; code and tests take
precedence, and the pack needs a separate factual correction. Every Russian line here is `DRAFT`.

## Titles used by feed and last album page

| Ending type | English `ENDING_TITLE` | Russian draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stopped | `She stopped after school` | `После школы она остановилась` |
| college | `She went to college` | `Она поступила в университет` |
| bankruptcy | `The money ran out` | `Деньги закончились` |
| injury | `The body stopped first` | `Первым не выдержал организм` |
| natural | `She played until she was done` | `Она играла, пока сама не решила закончить` |
| plateau | `She had gone as far as she was going` | `Дальше она идти не хотела` |
| peak | `She left at the top` | `Она ушла на вершине` |
| fall | `She stopped after the fall` | `После спада она остановилась` |
| family | `She did not go back` | `Она не вернулась в тур` |

The title must not grade the daughter or console the parent. The plateau title does not mean an
objective biological ceiling: the engine gate is her own reading of three stagnant seasons.
`Дальше она идти не хотела` risks sounding like lack of will; owner-read alternative:
`Она решила: дальше – не её путь`. Neither should claim a diagnosed limit.

The current English `stopped` detail itself consoles (`nobody had to call it a failure`) despite
the ending-copy rule against consolation; the Russian draft retains that source meaning for
line-by-line fidelity, but this is an **owner wording question**. More neutral alternative:
`После школы она решила остановиться. На этом её путь в турнирах закончился.` The owner may
choose a new canonical source line before the localization is wired. The natural-final row is
her statement, not a parental question. The peak detail uses exactly the clause that opened the
door; a title-only exit must not claim she was ranked №1.

## Her voice when she decides to leave

`leavingLine(door, temperament)` reads birth temperament for **expression only**. It has four
voices at the peak and four after the fall. None is a causal rule for *who* leaves. These are
kept milestone rows, not the parent's paraphrase on a button.

| Door / voice | English source | Russian draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| peak, fiery | `She said she was stopping at the top, and that was the whole conversation.` | `Сказала, что уходит на вершине. На этом разговор закончился.` |
| peak, quiet | `She said she was stopping here, while it was still good, and she did not make a thing of it.` | `Сказала, что остановится здесь, пока всё хорошо. Без объявления, почти между делом.` |
| peak, deep | `She said she had known for a while, and that she waited until the season was over so it would be finished and not just decided.` | `Сказала, что давно об этом думала. Дождалась конца сезона, чтобы не просто решить, а довести его до конца.` |
| peak, sunny | `She said she was going to go and have the rest of her life, and she sounded like someone with plans.` | `Сказала, что теперь хочет заняться остальной жизнью. Судя по голосу, планы у неё уже есть.` |
| fall, fiery | `She said she was not going to be watched losing it back, and she said it once.` | `Сказала, что не собирается проигрывать завоёванное на глазах у всех. Один раз сказала.` |
| fall, quiet | `She said she was stopping, and she said it as if it were something you already knew.` | `Сказала, что заканчивает. Так, будто мы и без неё это знали.` |
| fall, deep | `She said she had been turning it over all season, and that the season had only told her what she already thought.` | `Сказала, что обдумывала это весь сезон. Его итог лишь подтвердил то, к чему она сама пришла.` |
| fall, sunny | `She said she was glad she had done it, and that she did not want to spend the next year getting it back.` | `Сказала, что рада всему, что успела сделать. Ещё один год на возвращение прежнего места тратить не хочет.` |

The fiery peak line must stay short. The quiet lines make no invented scene. The deep lines
report what **she said**, not an omniscient truth about when she decided. The sunny peak line
does not claim she will keep playing tennis after the career ends.

Round 48 #4 (07.10, [RU-18](ru-main-delta-2-2026-10.md)): the one-more-year lines now credit the
**parent** – `One more year, you said.` and `You have said one more year {count} time/times` –
because it was the parent who answered. Only the diary row above needed its Russian subject
moved (`она` to the family `мы`, the owner's formula); the final-offer row's `прозвучало` names no
sayer, so its Russian stands as drafted. What stays **hers** is unchanged: `Nobody asked her this
time. She said it herself, and she said it steadily.`

The reserved place is **not departure**; the final junior season still runs before the academic
year. The professional row does not promise every entry pays a cheque. The return row grants the
right to enter again, not a guaranteed ranking or match. Error paths such as
`LAST_OFFER_NOT_A_QUESTION` need the same localization boundary as `StoreError`; they are not
normal beat dialogue.

`lastWordLine` is shared by the final offer's feed row and `RetirementDialog.vue`; runtime
localization must not fork those into two independently edited sentences. The final line is
her statement, never an invitation for the parent to overrule it. The plateau offer is a
spoken position, not a diagnosis that she objectively lacks talent.

## Not-currently-rendered copy

`ENDING_BLURB` contains nine authored English paragraphs but has no runtime consumer in `src/`
(the source/test search on 06.10 finds only declaration and test references). It is **not** a
current player-facing surface and is therefore not silently counted as localized. If a future
epilogue wires it in, that change needs a separate nine-paragraph editorial pass and a rendered
Russian catalogue; English must not become the fallback. The source's comment saying the
blurb is “live” conflicts with the usage search and with
`tests/component/wave8-family-ending.test.ts`'s explicit unrendered finding.

**Update 10.10 – this is no longer true.** On the owner's ask (item 34) the epilogue now draws all nine,
one paragraph under the ending's title on the last page (`EndingScreen.vue`, read through the declared
seat `ending.blurb`). Their nine rows, with the Russian column empty because the words are his, are in
[RU-12A](ru-ending-screen-2026-10.md) under «The ending's own paragraph». The condition above is
therefore not met yet: until a Russian cell is approved, a Russian session shows the English sentence
on that line, like any other unapproved row. Whether to hold the line back in Russian until then is his
call before a Russian release.
