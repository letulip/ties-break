# RU-09B – Journey-home diary corpus

## 1. Contract

These are scraps in the parent's hand under the journey painting. They stay third-person, warm and
small: a loss receives company, never a consolation prize; an injury receives tenderness, not a
diagnosis. Russian selection preserves every `TravelClaims` licence and the exact deterministic
index within the eligible pool.

`road` means car or bus; `car` alone licenses a back seat, car park or roadside stop. `air` means
airport or plane; `abroad` means a border and says nothing about the vehicle. `familyHomeVoice` and
`independentVoice` determine how the parent knows what happened after arrival – they are never
collapsed into an age check.

All Russian copy is `DRAFT`. Rows within each table follow source order one-for-one.

observation is why the source has a separate `wonTwo` claim.
