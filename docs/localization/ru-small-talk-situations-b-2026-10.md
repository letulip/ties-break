# RU-10B – Small-talk situation corpus, second continuation

This volume continues after [RU-10A](ru-small-talk-situations-2026-10.md). Source order, situation
IDs, subject, stage and fact gates follow the generated `src/engine/world/smallTalkCorpus.ts`;

## 1. Situation R19 – `the-name-she-heard`

Subject `worry`; stages `college`, `independent`; no fact gate. She heard her name but not the
conversation. None of the voices knows what was said.

## 2. Situation R20 – `the-money-she-did-not-ask-about`

Subject `worry`; stages `college`, `independent`; fact gate `march-entry-open`. The gate proves
that an eligible March entry is open and the deadline has not passed, but says nothing about the
entry's price. The gate excludes active college careers, so `college` in the row's stage list does
not imply the conversation fires during an active college program.

### 2.1 Openers

| voice | English source | Russian draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sunny | `"I haven't asked about the entry because I didn't want to make it a whole thing."` | `«Я не спрашивала о заявке, потому что не хотела устраивать из этого большой разговор».` |
| fiery | `"I'm not going to ask what it costs. I'd rather not know, and then I can't argue about it."` | `«Не стану спрашивать, сколько это стоит. Лучше не знать – тогда и спорить не о чем».` |
| deep | `"The entry's been open a while and I haven't mentioned it once."` | `«На турнир уже можно заявиться, а я всё молчу».` |
| quiet | `"The entry's open. I haven't put my name down."` | `«Приём заявок открыт. Я ещё не заявилась».` |

### 2.2 Shared stance labels

| stance | English source | Russian draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| invite | `Ask whether she wants the entry` | `Спросить, хочет ли она заявиться` |
| respond | `Say you can look at the numbers together` | `Предложить вместе посмотреть расходы` |
| space | `Say she needn't decide on this call` | `Сказать, что не обязательно решать всё в этом звонке` |

### 2.3 Replies

| voice | stance | English source | Russian draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sunny | invite | `"I do. I didn't want to say so before you knew what it cost."` | `«Хочу. Просто не хотела говорить, пока ты не узнаешь, сколько это стоит».` |

the runtime gate verifies only that the window is open now. No Russian answer invents a fee.
