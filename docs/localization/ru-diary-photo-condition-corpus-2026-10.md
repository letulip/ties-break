# RU-09C – Diary photo and condition corpus

## 1. Contract

The Home photograph and condition card each select at most one line from `DIARY_POOL`. Russian keeps
the source order, surface, claims and licence of every entry. A line that is warm but placed under
the wrong roof/away stage is still wrong.

All copy is `DRAFT`. This first section covers birthday, fresh results, injury onset/rehab, fatigue,
low-composure and exam photo captions. Vacation, ordinary-life and condition captions follow.
