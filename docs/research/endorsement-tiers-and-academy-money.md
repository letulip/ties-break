# The real endorsement tiers, and what an academy earns

**The research round 29 part four P6/P7 ordered, written BEFORE any number is picked.** The owner's
order, verbatim: «разнотировые рекламные контракты с реальными суммами, можешь сделать ресерч на эти
суммы для общего понимания и калибровки», and for the academy: «Посчитать сколько должна приносить
академия или поискать в интернете на примере Надаля». His anchor, which is P6's ruling: «Федерер
получал контракт с Nike на 10+ миллионов, это 1-2млн для родителя. Таких контрактов может быть
несколько.»

**Every number below is tagged.** `[S]` = stated in the cited source, read here rather than quoted
from a summary of it. `[I]` = inferred or computed here from sourced inputs, with the arithmetic
shown. `[GAP]` = looked for and not found; a stated gap is worth more than a plausible invention.
`[WEAK]` = a figure that circulates but whose trail ends at an aggregator or an unread snippet.
Where only a portfolio total is published and not per-deal values, the split is tagged `[I]` with
the arithmetic shown – his session permission («можно домыслить оставшееся») covers exactly that
case and nothing more.

This page EXTENDS [off-court-money.md](off-court-money.md) – this week's census, which established
that off-court money is not ordered by ranking, that no published non-endemic value exists below
roughly the top 25, and that kit deals run 8–10 years while non-endemic deals run 1–3. It also
leans on [tennis-academies.md](tennis-academies.md) (what academies charge) and the round-29
reachability report ([round-29.md](../rounds/round-29.md)). It builds the two things neither had:
the TIER TABLE and the ACADEMY'S OWN ACCOUNTS.

---

> **THE THREE ANSWERS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH.**
> **(1) The real ladder has four documented bands and they are separated by multiplication, not
> addition.** A top-10 icon banks **$15M–$25M a year off court** across **8–20 concurrent deals**;
> a second circle of top-10-adjacent names banks **$3M–$10M** across **4–8 deals**; the visible
> floor of publishable deals is roughly the top 25; below that a value has never been published –
> the census's `[GAP]`, re-searched and re-confirmed here down to a sponsor's own press release
> that names no number. The one lifetime figure in the sport: Sharapova retired with **$325M**, of
> which ~**$287M** was off-court, across an 11-year reign as the highest-paid woman in sport.
> **(2) The academy's answer is better than the question.** Nadal's own academy – his named
> example – published real accounts for 2023: **€40M revenue, €6.8M net profit**, and a stake sale
> that valued the whole at ~**€209M**, seven years after opening on a **€40M** build. But the split
> is the finding: programmes+lodging are 56% of revenue, and the academy runs **its own sponsorship
> line (€5.5M/yr)**, merch (€4.1M), restaurants and a gym – the TENNIS is the brand, the CAMPUS is
> the business. The tennis-programme company alone was barely profitable for its first seven years.
> **(3) The bridge: the shipped top advertising rung ($55,000) is 73× smaller than one documented
> mid-icon non-endemic deal, 155–182× smaller than one top kit deal, and 273–455× smaller than a
> real top-10 portfolio year.** At the owner's 10–20% commission, a real icon portfolio hands the
> parent **$1.5M–$5M a year** – his «1-2млн для родителя» sentence is literally the 10–20% of one
> $10M kit deal. Held for a real reign, that commission alone finishes the game's $12M academy in
> 3–8 seasons; the $38M long-range plane through commission alone requires a Sharapova – the single
> best-documented off-court career in the sport's history – and nothing shorter.

## 2. The portfolio mechanics – what P6's «таких контрактов может быть несколько» looks like in life

* **Concurrency is the norm at the top and it stacks by category exclusivity**: a watch house, a
  bank, an airline, a beauty house, a car – one of each, never two watches `[I]` from the
  composition of every portfolio in §1a (no portfolio read for this page holds two brands of the
  same trade at once).
* **The kit deal is the anchor tenant**: largest single value ($8.5–10M/yr at the top `[S]`),
  longest term (8–10yr), signed young (Gauff at 14, Raducanu at 15 `[S]`). Non-endemic deals
  arrive AFTER results and churn every 1–3 years – Raducanu's Vodafone and Porsche both lapsed
  within four years `[S]`; Świątek's own partner page no longer lists two much-reported earlier
  houses `[I]` from `[S]` (her site, 08.2026). **Churn is the variety** – round-29 (d)'s revision
  stands.
* **The ask is counted in days, and at the top it consumes the calendar**: the census's contract
  template prices appearances and shoot days; Zheng's word for the reality at 15 deals is that the
  off-season IS the shoot season `[S]`. The game's `shootWeeks` currency (2/4/6 a year) is the
  same thing at the same order of magnitude – six weeks ≈ 30 working days ≈ two days per deal
  across a 15-deal portfolio `[I]`.
* **Equity is the new top of the ask**: Osaka (BodyArmor, Hyperice), Sabalenka (IM8 shareholder),
  Gauff (Naked, with a title) all take stakes `[S]`/`[WEAK]` – beyond the game's scope, recorded
  so nobody mistakes the cash column for the whole compensation.

## 4. The calibration bridge – the game's numbers against the bands

The game-side inputs, verified in code and in the round-29 reachability report
(`tools/sponsor-ladder-reach.ts`, 72 careers × 780 weeks): ad ladder **$20k / $40k / $55k** at
WTA ≤200/≤50/≤10, 2/4/6 shoot weeks (`ECONOMY.advertising.houses`); the whole advertising post =
**0.67% of career money at the median, 1.92% at p90**; the academy = 4 stages summing **$12M**,
no upkeep, reachable by 29% of careers (21/72) at the shipped split and 19% (14/72) under a 10%
commission; the long-range plane = **$38,000,000** entry + 8%/yr upkeep = **$58,462/wk** ≈ $3.04M/yr
`[I]`, reached by **0 of 72**.

### 4b. Which real band, held how long, pays which shelf rung – through the commission alone

⭐ **The one documented career that clears the plane through commission alone is Sharapova's**:

upkeep, after an ELEVEN-YEAR reign as the highest-paid woman in sport. The game's own measurement
agrees from the other side: 0 of 72 careers reach it. **The rung is priced for the best off-court
career in the history of the sport; that is what «aspiration» costs, and P5's ruling says that is
the design.**

## §7 – the portfolio is CATEGORIES, not abstract slots (owner, 29.08)

His data point, Bublik's actual sponsor list: **одежда и обувь · ракетки и экипировка · часы ·
