## §1. The booth (C4) – one merged taxonomy, five disciplines

The two tennis sources agree to a striking degree. Merged line-kinds, each with its licence:

1. **Consequence-first score wraps** – almost never a bare score; each call carries the stake.
2. **Ledger-keeping** – elapsed time as drama, streak collisions, saved-match-point tallies.
3. **Body-reads WITH A HEDGE** – mind inferred from the visible (serve speed down, wide misses)
   and the inference MARKED (question form, «it would appear»).
4. **Baseline memory** – the booth keeps behavioural norms across weeks and calls the deviation
   («the first head-shake of the entire tournament»).
5. **Form-and-biography at maximum leverage** – the old collapse story told exactly before the
   big point; commentary as antagonist-within-bounds.
6. **The box-read** – the camera cuts to the players' box, the booth annotates with a one-line
   biography rider. ⭐ This is the booth's ONE legitimate window onto our family, and it matches
   his 10.09 publicity ruling exactly: a REVEALED fact at a fame that makes her news.
7. **Precedent and archive** – today measured against the vault; superlatives rationed, then
   spent all at once at the end.
8. **Folk law and superstition** – stated as law, not opinion (a rain break favours the man who
   is losing).
9. **The theory essay** – a two-voice disquisition on how choking begins: ⭐ the booth is allowed
   to state our spirit model AS PUNDIT FOLKLORE, never as telemetry.
10. **Personal asides** – ex-player memories, self-implication, dry jokes over dead air, the odd
    flagged speculation about an absent person's feelings. The cigar family lives here.
11. **Banter and disagreement** – skeptic vs empath; play settles it; ⭐ **the booth may be WRONG
    and perform an on-air reversal** – wrongness plus graceful reversal is a genre move, not a
    defect, and it is what makes an outside voice honest over a hidden stat.
12. **Procedure-when-it-bites, crowd, weather, production housekeeping** – the booth is a
    workplace; dead-air register (procedural trivia in a rain delay) is its own genre.

**The five disciplines to build into C4:** (a) stake-indexed COMPRESSION – more drama, fewer
words, silence at the summit; (b) hedge-markers on every mind-read; (c) baseline memory; (d) the
publicity edge – speculate exactly to the edge of what a camera shows, never past it (Wimbledon's
booth learns the romance only from a face in the box and a champion searching the crowd); (e) the
right to be wrong and reverse. Plus the register split observed everywhere: booth = present
tense, no interiority; interviews = past tense, full interiority, word-searching; officials =
liturgy (fixed formulas that give a spirit spike something to break against).

## §2. Parents – pressure anatomies, fallibility, and the laws of repair

**Pressure moves (the beats' parent options, and what a mis-parented week sounds like):**
* **The deficit question** – a 96 met with «what about the other four?»; question → question →
  question until the child argues her own inadequacy. Pressure staged as curiosity.
* **Praise as door-closing** – affirmation deployed to END the child's complaint, not meet it
  («You did fine. That's everything» – the film's model of a well-meaning adult getting it
  wrong). ⭐ The fallible parent (B4) in one line.
* **Belief as a weapon** – the mother who pushes by BELIEVING (refuses the age excuse, diagnoses
  «great and afraid»); a pressure-parent need not demand.
* **Schedule as the instrument of control** – Bradbury interrupts by calendar (an interview in N
  minutes, a dawn flight); prohibition delivered as anaphora (stop looking / calling / coming
  around); love argued as SKILL DECAY (footwork off, serve a mess); the trap of making the
  daughter voice the prohibition herself.
* **Litigating over the child's head** – adults fight her case in front of her, unaddressed.
* **Grievance mirroring** – a bid for care answered with the parent's own hardship.
* **Care as tactics** – intimacy conducted entirely through the shared campaign; the tell is a
  pronoun (the plan ends in first-person plural). Both warm AND a warning.

**The laws of repair (validates and sharpens who-she-is §2a):**
* **Repair is sufficiency, not a cleared ledger** – Tenenbaums repairs nearly everything while
  the parent grows almost not at all; the film ends at ENOUGH. Our «repair is free, growth is
  work», decoupled exactly.
* ⚠ **Counterfeit free repair collapses on audit** – the faked illness. «Free» means LOW-COST
  SINCERE ACTS (presence, status-costly gestures, asking factual questions about the wound),
  never zero-cost claims.
* ⚠ **Walls spike hardest at a rejected OFFERING** – the dismissed birthday play. Absence
  wounds; a critiqued offering scars. Our asked/given machinery already carries this seam.
* **Crisis is an amnesty window** – grudge protocols suspend during emergencies and partially
  re-arm; concessions made inside the window partly persist.
* **Verbosity asymmetry is the meter** – the wronged party speaks less, the guilty floods;
  repair progress = the parent's word count falling toward the child's. ⭐ Mechanisable.
* **The deferred reply** – a question from beat k answered in beat k+n; the latency shrinking
  IS the thaw, felt without one warmer word. ⭐ Mechanisable.
* **The deferred invoice** – a containment contract made in childhood (emotion relocated inward)
  is billed years later. The four-beat arc: verdict → adults litigate over the child → the
  contract → the invoice. A ready long-arc for the walls model.

## §3. Her voices – the content-separation rules (B1–B2, the flat pool)

* **Distinguish temperaments by**: what she notices · turn length · question-to-declarative
  ratio · WHERE meta-commentary appears – never adjectives, never punctuation crutches.
* **Fluency tracks armor** – characters stammer exactly when out of persona; the private one's
  silence is a default STATE, the expressed one's silence is an EVENT.
* **Closeness shortens lines** – the most intimate scenes run one-to-three-word turns over
  shared referents; institutional speech balloons. Warmth is brevity, not sweetness.
* ⭐ **The code-shift wall** – the film keeps Borg's intimate life in another LANGUAGE than his
  public life. A private girl's close register can be a code shift (lexicon, rhythm), not merely
  fewer words.
* **The `deep` disclosure budget** – roughly one true sentence per crisis, purchasable only by a
  direct question, delivered flat at maximum stakes, often with a liability waiver attached.
* **The flat-pool rule** – the flatter the line, the more CONCRETE its one detail must be, and
  the bigger the off-screen fact it must sit on (wrong-scale precision; repetition without
  variation; formal diction between intimates as negotiated distance).
* **A declaration never travels alone** – wrap it in another speech act (a scold, a broadcast, a
  bet); the first one is misdelivered, the real one must be asked back.
* **The private character discloses by choosing which mislabel to fight** («I'm not a machine»
  – the one correction volunteered all film).
* **The quadrant casts** – Wimbledon alone seats all four corners (steady-fading lead ·
  expressed-intense lead · open-steady friend · closed-intense rival); Borg's cast fills the
  same grid. Independent confirmation the two axes carry a whole ensemble.

## §4. Romance inside tour logistics (waves 3–4, step 6)

The layer's thesis confirmed at feature length: **the film never invents a romantic occasion –
every beat is a tournament mechanism repurposed.** The move-list:
* arrival as an administrative accident (lodging, draws, schedules – our episodes' world);
* escalation as challenge-loops inside training (flirtation in drill currency);
* progress result-gated and MACHINE-MEDIATED (voicemail, switchboards, TV sets carry more
  traffic than faces – our feed/knownWeek machinery is this);
* the secrecy pact declared in PERFORMANCE terms (a boyfriend degrades her first serve), so
  every later breach is legible;
* the break-up as performance divergence (he wins, she loses, same day) – the loss converted
  into causation, scoring vocabulary bent into relational vocabulary;
* repair = PUBLIC GESTURE + a WINDOW ONLY THE CALENDAR CAN OPEN (the rain delay) + an
  INFORMATION GIFT (her scouting read powers his comeback) – intel-plus-morale, not sentiment;
* object proxies and the wrong custodian (a confession lodged with a side character is a time
  bomb); confessions in parallel body positions, not face to face; ⭐ **suspension as a valid
  resolution** (mutual-known-unacted is a stable end-state);
* marriage happens OUTSIDE the structure (epilogue register) – the tournament hosts arrival,
  rupture and gesture, never the wedding. Step 6 should keep that grain;
* closure by STATE-CHANGE, not dialogue: attendance, access, results and the ledger end arcs
  that lines opened.

## §5. Coach and staff (C2, the psychologist)

* **«Return her to her own game»** – the crisis line that names no error, gives no tactic, and
  restores identity. The model changeover sentence.
* **Inventory together** – co-performed ritual instead of conversation as the regulation
  channel.
* **Mantra economy** – mantras are short, old, and framed as PREREQUISITES («good face» family);
  never fresh, never long.
* **The vow move** – split the outcome from the surrender: recovery = reclaiming the
  controllable half. The psychologist's recovery focus in one shape.
* **Doubt as alertness** – player and coach hold the doctrine independently; doubt keeps you
  improving. Cool-head copy should never promise doubt's removal.
* **The fitness coach is a covert psychologist** – a third of sessions is talk; the identity
  thesis (the person must embody the player). Our psychologist's fiction has precedent.
* **Evaluation as a gap** – talent phrased as the distance between what she holds and what she
  can yet express; kind and patient at once. A coach-note shape.
* **One script-break per fixture character** – the locker-room attendant runs the same scripted
  encouragement for everyone until ONE crisis, then breaks script once. Maximum value, minimum
  pool.

## §6. Institutions (C3)

## Chapter A — *Strokes of Genius* (2018): the booth, temperament, respect, pressure

**Load-bearing fact about the film's architecture:** it has **no narrator**. Archival broadcast
audio *is* the narration spine; the chair umpire's calls are the metronome; interviews carry all
interiority. That three-way split is itself the biggest structural borrow available to us.

### A1. The booth

Observed grammar of a call: **official's tick → one sentence of consequence → optional colour**,
with compression increasing as stakes rise. Catalogue of kinds, each with what licenses it:

| Kind | License (what the booth can SEE/KNOW) | The move |
|---|---|---|
| Scene-setting small talk | sky, calendar, tradition | Weather and ritual open the day – and double as foreshadowing; rain later returns as plot, and the booth points at the sky like a neighbour |
| Consequence-first score wrap | scoreboard + known stakes | Almost no bare scores: each call carries what it means for the mountain |
| Ledger-keeping | countables | Elapsed time as drama (first lead after 3h37); streak collision (neither man has lost a tiebreak – so something must give); saved match points tallied aloud |
| Body-read with hedge | posture, pace, face | Infers mind from body but **marks the inference** (question form; «it would appear»). Peak form: deviation-from-baseline – first head-shake *of the entire tournament* |
| Box-read + relationship rider | camera cut to the players' box | The uncle who can't watch, plus a one-line biography rider. This is how family legally enters a broadcast: director cuts, booth annotates |
| Precedent / archive | shared tennis history | Today measured against the vault; superlatives are *rationed* until the end, then spent all at once |
| Procedure-when-it-bites | rulebook | Rules explained only at the moment they become plot |
| Folk law / superstition | communal fan belief | Stated as law, not opinion: a rain break favours the man who's losing; «Today's the day» (booth; illustration – never to lift) |
| Crowd-read | audible hall | An eruption, not a cheer; desire read aloud, with a politeness rider toward the man it disadvantages |
| Personal aside / speculative empathy | the booth's own chair; flagged imagination | Dry jokes covering dead air; first-person pleasure declared on air; imagining an absent character's feelings, explicitly flagged as speculation |

**Density and rhythm around big points.** As stakes rise, words shrink: big-point grammar
collapses to interjection + one clause, then silence for the crowd. At the summit, analysis
abdicates on principle – McEnroe (interview) says prescribing tactics then would be nonsense;
the statistician recalls thinking stats had gone irrelevant. The top of the register is an
instruction to *watch*. Post-match has its own mode: the gentle losing question, consolation
offered and honestly quantified – Federer accepts it helps «a little bit» (illustration – never
to lift).

**C4 mapping.** «Может быть что угодно в текстовой трансляции» is confirmed – but every «что
угодно» in a real booth belongs to a license class. Rebuild C4 as this taxonomy plus three
disciplines: hedge-markers on any mind-read; a stake-indexed compression rule; a baseline memory
so it can call deviations. The box-read row is C4's only legitimate window onto our family.

### A2. Temperament as content

- **Federer (steady-presenting, high-openness speech):** metaphor stacks (career as puzzle, as
  chess); aesthetic noticing (the scissors-perfect grass); self-surprise at his own talent;
  second-person self-address at emotional peaks; asks the interviewer to help him find a word.
  His confessions are arcs: past flaw → announced decision to change. Emotion arrives processed,
  retrospective, often joked.
- **Nadal (intense-ritualistic speech):** doubt-as-alertness credo; refusal of prediction (asked
  how to beat Federer on grass: no idea – only my best, my rhythm, my intensity, then
  congratulate him); inventory logic; feelings reported as short *events*, unelaborated; one
  confessed exception – a victory thought at championship point, followed by the double-fault
  premonition.

**Where the mapping onto our axes breaks (important):** (1) the film insists the poles converge
– scratch the surface and they're quite similar; both self-describe as problem-solvers. The axes
govern *register*, not underlying drive. (2) Nadal's ritual is framed by his own camp as
comfort-*engineering* (a programmed household, a rented «home») – only the booth frames it as
quirk. Two valid external voices for one trait. (3) Federer's steadiness is historicized as
*converted* volatility – the racket-throwing junior is on the record. Temperament presentation
can evolve mid-career, and the archive stays quotable: exactly the license for our booth and
psychologist to contrast a daughter's present composure with her catalogued past.

### A3. How pros talk about each other – moves

1. **Credential the compliment with your own scars**, then admit the fear it causes.
2. **Elevate the rival to necessity or fate.**
3. **Counterfactual gratitude** – fewer titles because of you, better player because of you;
   respect priced in what you'd pay.
4. **Scoreboard humility** – the winner re-crowns the loser in numbers, not adjectives.
5. **The impossible-draw wish** – honoring the rival by wishing the zero-sum away.
6. **Concession + rain-check** – he deserved it, and you deserve one too.
7. **Praise via mechanism, not epithet**; and **duty-to-admire** (whoever doesn't rate him
   doesn't know tennis).

### A4. Interview vs booth vs narration

- **Booth:** present tense, no future knowledge, no interiority – inference from the visible.
- **Interview:** past tense with full interiority, unfinished sentences, self-correction. Its
  signature device: **memory arrives as re-enacted dialogue** – people perform their own
  remembered lines (Toni whispering «Double fault» to the agent beside him – illustration, never
  to lift). Family history in our sim should surface the same way: a parent quoting their own
  old sentence, not summarizing it.
- **The official:** the umpire's proud procedural *we*; inside the event, outside both camps.
- **Borrows:** interiority firewall; tense discipline as voice separation; re-enacted speech as
  the family-memory format.

### A5. Pressure talk – moves for the psychologist/spirit layer

1. **Doubt reframed as alertness** – held independently by player and coach.
2. **The vow: split outcome from surrender** – «Federer could win, but I wasn't going to lose»
   (Nadal, interview, translated; illustration – never to lift). Recovery = reclaiming the
   controllable half. The model move of the whole film.
3. **Interruption reframed as alarm clock** – the rain delay credited with waking him; the reset
   goal is process-shaped.
4. **The locker room as resettling chamber** – talk to the team, stop panicking.
5. **Premonition is real and leaks sideways** – spoken to a neighbour in the box, never to the
   player mid-match.
6. **Mantra economy** – two words, «Good face» (illustration – never to lift), repeated for
   years: attitude as precondition for learning. Mantras are short, old, prerequisites.
7. **Pressure scales with unrepeatability, not prize.**
8. **Honest grief timeline** – worst-day hyperbole in the moment; the magnitude lands weeks
   later; consolation helps only a little, and saying so is allowed.

### A6. Parents and teams

- **The thesis is our premise:** behind every champion there is a force – a father, a mother, a
  grandmother, a coach (Navratilova's formulation).
- **Toughness as deliberate parental design** (Toni): trained him as if each practice were a
  final; deliberately fostered a personality that could withstand difficulty; polices humility.
  Parenting presented as engineering, with the child later co-signing it.
- **The shame register** (Federer's parents): ashamed courtside; discipline via withdrawal
  threat; the mother's tactical reframe – a tantrum is an invitation to your opponent. ⭐ The
  currency: parents argue in the sport's own logic, not just morality.
- **A staged morale debate:** the young player believes venting helps (citing McEnroe as
  precedent); coaches counter that negative energy costs points – our exact morale loop, with
  the child quoting role models in her own defense.
- **Fitness coach as covert psychologist** (Paganini): a third of sessions is talk; the
  identity-integration thesis (the man must embody the player).
- **Home as battery:** normalcy defended by the family; on tour the household is rebuilt and
  «programmed» for calm – comfort as logistics, run largely by the family.
- **A coach-voice line-shape worth keeping:** talent visible before it can be expressed –
  evaluation phrased as the gap between содержание and выражение, kind and patient at once.

## Chapter B — *Borg vs McEnroe* (2017): parent pressure, temperament fingerprints, refusals

**Source condition, load-bearing:** the English-subtitle transcript – the Swedish-language Borg
family/Bergelin intimacy layer survives only as stage markers (knocks, doors, sighs, retching, a
TV switched off). Where the chapter describes Swedish-language content it relies on the film
generally and says so; zero quotes come from those scenes. Quote budget used: 9, all marked.

### B1. Parent–child pressure

**The McEnroe household (fully in English – the transcript's richest parent material):**
- *The interrogation scene (geography test).* The father never asserts; he only asks. A 96 meets
  «What about the other four?» (father; illustration – never to lift), then the achievement is
  relativized, then the passion becomes the suspect. **Escalation: question → question →
  question, each smaller-aimed, until the child argues his own inadequacy.** Pressure staged as
  curiosity; the adult's hands stay clean. Payoff: the word *focus* resurfaces years later in
  the adult's on-court grievance language – the parent's vocabulary becomes the player's.
- *The mismatch scene.* Adult John rages about a broken promise; the father answers a different
  scene: «You did fine. That's everything.» (father; illustration – never to lift).
  **Support-shaped dismissal: praise deployed to close the complaint rather than meet it.**
- *The gatekeeper call.* A bid for care met with a counter-bid – **the parent competes for
  hardship acknowledgment instead of receiving it.**
- *The one working channel (chess).* Intimacy only through tactics; the tell is a pronoun – the
  plan ends in first-person plural: the parent has merged with the campaign.
- *The failure to protect (billboard).* The parent's objection is staged as a question that
  loses to money logic in one sentence.

**The Borg side (structure-from-markers + film knowledge, labeled):** the ban/racket episode
runs **verdict → adults litigate over the boy's head while he is present but unaddressed → one
adult offers a CONTRACT instead of a verdict** (the emotion is not banned – it is relocated
inward, into the shots) **→ the contract's hidden price is billed years later** (rituals, the
retching, the near-mute privacy). What the adults get wrong: reading intensity as a character
defect; what the coach gets wrong: pricing containment as free. A four-beat shape directly
borrowable for a life-beat chain.

### B2. Temperament as content

- **Borg = private+intense presenting as private+steady; the mask is the plot.** Fingerprint:
  verdict-first flat declaratives, no reasons («No special feelings.» – Borg, presser;
  illustration – never to lift). Notices order and sameness; omits himself. Volunteers exactly
  one correction all film – when the label is wrong: «I'm not a machine.» (Borg, presser;
  illustration – never to lift). **The private character discloses by choosing which mislabel
  to fight.** Inward intensity leaks somatically, never lexically.
- **McEnroe = open+intense.** Torrential second person, question barrages, meta-commentary on
  the exchange itself. Notices sound and attention-distribution (pigeons, whose name an
  interview circles). Keeps a fairness ledger. Omits: apology, and other people's interiority.
  His one sincere self-disclosure is delivered as an attack on the press.
- **Silence, asymmetric:** Borg's silence is a default state; McEnroe's silence is an EVENT.
  Both leads stammer only when out of persona. **Fluency tracks armor.**
- **Supporting cast fills the quadrants:** Gerulaitis open+steady (the social translator –
  «Really he's a volcano keeping it all in until boom.», illustration – never to lift); Fleming
  private+steady. Extracted rule: distinguish temperaments by *what they notice, turn length,
  question-to-declarative ratio, and where meta-commentary appears* – never adjectives.
- ⭐ **Language itself as the privacy wall:** the film keeps Borg's intimate life in another
  language than his public life. Game analog: a private daughter's close register can be a
  *code shift*, not merely fewer words.

### B3. Coach voice (Bergelin)

Registers reconstructable: **(a) logistical/ritual** – the nightly racket inventory, regulation
performed *with* the athlete through shared procedure; **(b) crisis** – physical proximity plus
near-silence (enter, sit, wait); **(c) restorative** – the one English changeover line: «Just
play your tennis.» (changeover; illustration – never to lift) – names no error, gives no tactic,
hands the player back his own identity. **Moves:** «return him to his own game» · «inventory
together» · «defend outward, demand inward».

### B4. What is refused to be said

- **Borg refuses:** the history/stakes frame; adjectives about himself except the one he
  corrects; the rival's televised praise (staged as a device action – TV off); the *why* behind
  a need («It has to be the right one.» – door scene; illustration – never to lift), explanation
  withheld; celebrity itself (the alias scene).
- **McEnroe refuses:** apology – staged spectacularly: alone, he whispers both options and
  chooses the armor; every behavior question; the empathy-frame for Borg; the compliment inside
  Fleming's prophecy (hears only the insult).
- **Institutions refuse:** error (the umpire re-asserts); temperament itself (court audio turned
  down by order – muting as policy).
- ⭐ **The staging of avoidance:** refusals are almost never verbal «no»s – they are FORMAT
  CHANGES: exit, device-off, register-switch to tactics, a joke, a door. Endings refuse speech
  entirely (two «no audible dialogue» beats at the emotional peak).

### B5. Press / institutions

Talk show: hostility laundered as entertainment; insults delivered deniably by quoting third
parties; the audience as an instrument. Press conference: trap questions repeated until
breakage; the pack speaks as one organism; Borg subtracts, McEnroe litigates the frame.
Commentary booth: three alternating registers – technical call → historical stakes → body/heart
metaphor; under tension sentences shorten to exclamations; a riff-phrase reused as callback;
numbers as incantation; epithet-forging and metaphor duels; moral scoring of temperament (the
loser praised as «beyond reproach»). Business register: intimacy priced; ethics flattened to a
number; temperament converted to brand («gentleman or rebel» as market segmentation).

### B6. Relationship moves (name → mechanics → location)

1. **Care as tactics** – affection lives in the plan's pronouns (father–son chess; McEnroe and
   Fleming over the draw poster).
2. **Praise as door-closing** (father, after the talk show).
3. **Deficit question** (geography test).
4. **Gossip as portrait** – the private character painted entirely by a third party's
   affectionate list of his rituals; the subject never confirms or denies (Gerulaitis).
5. **One-time truth** – «I'm gonna say this once.» (Fleming; illustration – never to lift);
   scarcity forces listening; deliver calm, leave before rebuttal.
6. **Rehearsed apology, chosen insult** – both responses voiced alone, the armored one picked.
7. **Assertion without explanation** – the anxious need stated as fact; compliance requested,
   not understanding (the door scene).
8. **Alias as vacation** – resting from being oneself under the FATHER's name: the fantasy of
   normalcy is a fantasy of being the parent.
9. **Permission-joke intimacy** – an embrace initiated under cover of a teasing question, so
   both can deny it mattered (the airport).
10. **Grievance mirroring** (the mother's call).
11. **Inventory together** (coach–player).
12. **Return him to his own game** (the changeover).

### B7. Escalation / interruption / asymmetry

- **Two opposite escalation engines.** Expressed-intense escalates by *adding opponents*:
  trigger → evidence claim → demand → meta-attack («You cannot be serious!» – McEnroe,
  semifinal; illustration – never to lift – is the apex of a five-step ramp) → widening the
  front. Contained-intense de-escalates by *subtraction*: shorter answers → none → exit;
  crises detonate offstage, somatically. A scene between the two types is a flood meeting a
  drain.
- **Interruption grammar by source:** institutions interrupt with time-words; intimates with
  knocks and doors; the expressed-intense character interrupts *himself* exactly when sincere.
- ⭐ **Turn length as intimacy meter** – the closest scenes run one-to-three-word turns;
  institutional speech balloons. Closeness should *shorten* lines, not sweeten them.
- **Deferred-invoice arc** – the film's whole spine (see B1).
- **The chorus** – crowd as a cued instrument either side can accidentally recruit.

## Chapter C — *The Royal Tenenbaums* (2001): estrangement, unspoken love, deadpan

⚠ **Provenance:** the source page was captcha-walled to the analyzing agent (bypass refused;
mirrors unreadable). This chapter is built from model knowledge of the film – scene details
high-confidence but UNVERIFIED against the exact transcript; quote budget cut to ONE.

### C1. Estrangement and repair: the failed parent's return

**Scene anatomy of the return, as reusable functions:** intel via intermediary → pretext entry
(the fabricated illness) → a honeymoon of novelty that costs him nothing → audit and exposure
(the outsider-suitor fact-checks the illness) → expulsion → menial re-entry (a servant's job in
the hotel he once lived in – a voluntary status drop) → costly gives (the divorce finally
granted) → **crisis as amnesty window** (a suicide attempt, a wedding-day crash suspend the
grudge protocols) → micro-acknowledgments accepted → **death mid-repair**: full reconciliation
is never staged; the film ends at a SUFFICIENCY threshold. ⭐ Model repair as *reaching
sufficiency*, not *clearing the ledger*.

**Apology without the apology register:** (a) cheerful self-indictment – naming his own villainy
with relish disarms without requesting absolution; (b) logistics-as-love – papers, a replacement
dog, arranged outings: competence in service is his only sincere register; (c) **asking factual
questions about the wound** – the children respond far more to being *asked* than to being
apologized to; (d) the lie that becomes true mid-utterance – the faked week claimed as the best
of his life, certified sincere as spoken.

**What lands vs what bounces.** Bounces: retroactive condolence, general apologies,
self-justification, gifts aimed at his own guilt. Lands: status-costly acts, sustained presence
at a lower station, acknowledgment of one SPECIFIC wound (the lifelong «adopted» tag), presence
inside a crisis. The angriest child's entire thaw is six flat words – «I've had a rough year,
dad.» (Chas; illustration – never to lift) – **asymmetry itself: the parent spends thousands of
words, the child repays with six, and that IS the reconciliation.**

**Mapping to walls / «repair is free, growth is work» – validated, with a sharpening.** Royal
repairs nearly every relationship while growing almost not at all – the axes decouple exactly as
our model says, and growth is NOT required for repair. Sharpening: **counterfeit free repair
collapses on audit** – «free» must mean *low-cost sincere acts*, never *zero-cost claims*. And:
the daughter's walls originate at a REJECTED OFFERING (her childhood play, dismissed at her own
birthday) – **walls should spike hardest when a parent misses or critiques an offering beat**,
not merely when absent.

### C2. Richie, the tennis player: the public collapse

**Staging:** delivered as archival broadcast footage – narrated by strangers. Before: the cause
is SHOWN (a cutaway to the just-married sister in the players' box), never spoken. During: the
booth describes behavior in stat-language (shoes and one sock removed, tears, an absurd error
count) and **openly admits it cannot explain any of it – incomprehension is the chorus.** After:
a clinical narrator supplies numbers and dates. ⭐ Division of labor: *the public gets the
behavior, the audience gets the cause, the character gets silence* – stage a professional
collapse through a commentary layer that can only inventory conduct while the relationship layer
holds the cause; **the gap between the two narrations is the emotional payload.**

**Richie's speech (our `deep`/walls reference):** never volunteers cause or feeling; the reason
for the suicide attempt is spoken only when directly asked, minimally, with a liability waiver
attached. Agreement-first turns, short declaratives, logistics; displacement through objects and
acts (years of paintings of one never-named subject; a childhood tent re-pitched as the one
place where talk is possible; a falcon released as affairs-in-order in object language).
⭐ **A `deep` character's disclosure budget: roughly one true sentence per crisis, purchasable
only by a direct question.**

### C3. Love that cannot be spoken: moves

1. **Arrival as declaration** – blocking and timing spend the feeling; dialogue near zero.
2. **Object proxies** – one or two charged objects route the sentiment so lines don't have to.
3. **Wrong custodian** – a confession lodged with a side character is a time bomb, not relief.
4. **Asymmetric alliance** – the injured parties form an odd temporary pair to process her.
5. **Direct speech priced at near-death** – the only frontal exchange happens after the attempt,
   lying side by side, never face to face. Gate frank lines behind irreversible events; prefer
   parallel body positioning.
6. **The pact of deferral** – a stable end-state of *mutual-known-unacted*: a romance beat may
   resolve into permanent suspension.

### C4. The deadpan register (our flat pool / `quiet` voice)

Mechanics: understatement after catastrophe (six words as total accounting) · wrong-scale
precision (exact counts and brands attached to enormous events; a dossier read aloud in
monotone by a hired stranger – **the monotone bomb: maximum damage = neutral channel × private
content**) · formal diction between intimates (closeness as negotiated distance) · repetition
without variation (the identical «adopted» tag across decades – escalation via sameness) ·
displacement (narrator, music, props absorb what the line refuses) · peak-minus-one cutting
(the reply arrives scenes later or never). ⭐ **Rule for the flat pool: the flatter the line,
the more concrete its one detail must be, and the bigger the off-screen fact it must sit on.**

### C5. Supporting-cast economy: four satellites vs our four-trait rule

Pagoda (habit: knife-carrying spy-work · notices everything · friction: divided loyalty ·
effect: exists so the private planner can think aloud) · Henry Sherman (habit: accountant's
verification · notices fraud · friction: the anti-Royal · ⭐ effect: a **register-forcing
agent** – around him the charmer's speech drops performance and goes plain) · Raleigh (habit:
clinical study · notices his wife's withdrawal · friction: the injured party who keeps showing
up · effect: sets the ceiling on how quietly devastation may be spoken) · Dudley (habit:
literal-minded questions · notices – he FINDS the bleeding Richie · friction: obliviousness at
the wrong moments · effect: the rescue mediated by the least fluent speaker stays
unsentimental). **Verdict on §5c: validated – exactly one habit / notice / friction /
speech-effect each, and none of them arc.** Upgrades adopted: a signature OBJECT per satellite;
the notice may be plot-load-bearing (each satellite's noticing triggers exactly one event).

### C6. Escalation, interruption, asymmetry

**Ledger escalation** – grievances itemized, not dramatized; fights escalate by appending
receipts in flat voice · **event interruption as scene converter** – the car through the wall
kills the stalled ceremony and BECOMES the reconciliation venue: interruptions transpose beats,
not cancel them · ⭐ **the deferred reply** – questions from beat k answered in beat k+n, and
the latency shrinking is how thawing walls can be FELT mechanically · ⭐ **verbosity asymmetry
as power** – the wronged party speaks less; repair progress = the parent's word count falling
toward the child's · **narrator voicing the unsaid** – our analogue: stage-direction text may
carry the daughter's withheld content, explicitly marked unspoken · **crisis as amnesty
window** – concessions made inside it partially persist.

## Chapter D — *Wimbledon* (2004): romance in the draw, the booth, the inner voice

⚠ Source note: the transcript's markup drops all digits – ranks and scores reconstructed from
context. Quote budget deliberately held to ONE.

### D1. Romance inside tour logistics

The film never invents a romantic occasion; every beat is a tournament mechanism repurposed:
- **Arrival = administrative error** – a hotel upgrade puts his key into her suite; lodging
  stays a battlefield all film (an upgrade refused mid-streak for superstition; the father
  relocates her as a separation weapon; a dressing-room honor declined for ritual).
- **Escalation = practice-court wagering** – flirtation conducted in drill currency,
  hit-the-target bets stepping up stakes. Courtship literally uses the serve mechanic.
- **Progress is result-gated and machine-mediated** – she calls BECAUSE he won round one, via
  answering machine; voicemail, switchboards and TV sets carry more relationship traffic than
  faces.
- **The secrecy pact as state contract** – declared in PERFORMANCE terms (the father's doctrine
  that a boyfriend degrades her first serve), so every later breach is legible.
- **Complications are the calendar** – the draw pits him against his best friend; a sponsor
  party makes the affair public; the father's interruptions are always schedule-shaped.
- **The break-up is performance divergence** – same day he wins, she loses; the loss converted
  into causation, capped by the film's thesis pun: «Love means nothing in tennis. Zero. It only
  means you lose.» (Lizzie; illustration – never to lift). ⭐ Scoring vocabulary bent into
  relational vocabulary – cheap for us, because the lexicon is shared.
- **Repair needs three structural devices** – a hijacked press conference (the public apology
  reaching her through an airport TV), a rain delay (the only pause wide enough for a
  locker-room reunion), and an INFORMATION GIFT (her scouting read of the opponent's serve
  tells). Reconciliation delivered as intel-plus-morale; the comeback runs on her data.
- **Marriage happens outside the structure** – epilogue voiceover only; a background comet runs
  a second countdown parallel to the draw: the romance has its own clock.

### D2. The booth

Two-tier design: a measured host voice early, a duo (skeptic + empath) for the business end.
Line taxonomy: state narration · form-and-biography at maximum leverage (the old collapse story
before the big point) · psychological inference from visible symptoms only · ⭐ **the theory
essay** – a two-voice disquisition on how choking begins, laid over montage: the booth states
the sim's spirit model AS PUNDIT FOLKLORE · personal asides and self-implication · **banter
with reversal** – the skeptic writes a player off, the empath rebuts with observed evidence,
play settles it, and the skeptic performs an on-air 180 · crowd/weather · ethics-flagging
(naming gamesmanship) · production housekeeping. Rhythm at leverage: pre-frame → clipped
reaction → re-narrativization; at championship point the booth RAISES pressure by exhuming the
player's wound – commentary as antagonist within bounds. Dead-air register (rain delay
procedural trivia) is its own genre. ⭐ **Outside-knowledge discipline:** the booth learns the
romance only through public visibles – a celebrity in the players' box, a champion searching
the crowd – and speculates exactly to the edge of what a camera shows. The exact contract for
an outside-but-alive broadcaster over a hidden stat: name symptoms, guess causes, be wrong
sometimes, reverse gracefully. And the voice is a GENRE characters can borrow – the lovers
parody it on a derelict court; the father at a match is the anti-booth (same visible data,
second person, imperative, possessive).

### D3. Inner voice vs spoken voice (bounded spirit, as structure)

The inner voice is second-person and imperative at points, essayistic between them – two
registers, one speaker. **Doubt is staged as intrusive playback**: other people's remembered
lines replay DURING the point; recovery is noise-cancellation – a self-command, crowd sound
fading, one breath, the serve. Confidence swings are audio-mixing, not stat talk. Bounded
effect shown physically (fatigue monologue → dropped set); and the ceiling: even peak
confidence never plays the point – it only quiets the noise. ⭐ Even the confident inner voice
misbehaves – at match point it drifts into daydream and must self-interrupt: the inner voice
has its own discipline loop. Spirit spikes break REGISTER before they break play (politeness
collapses once into a code violation). POV asymmetry: only one character gets a monologue;
everyone else is inferred – matching a sim where only her spirit is instrumented.

### D4. Parent and family registers

The mother: brisk faith as pressure – belief is her weapon. The father: defeatist-tender,
recants his whole curriculum as a blessing; repaired THROUGH the son's run – family morale
flows uphill from results. ⭐ The brother as odds-tracker – a family member whose WAGERS are a
diegetic confidence meter. Superstition arc via invitation: never won with family attending →
disinvites them → after the belief shift, insists they come, renouncing luck: attendance as
the mechanical token of inner change. Earnestness smuggled under comedy – sincerity capped at
one line before deflection. **Dennis Bradbury (the protective father) – moves:**
interruption-by-schedule · fourfold anaphoric prohibition · the technical proxy argument (love
argued as skill decay) · the trap of making the daughter voice the prohibition herself ·
softening ENACTED, not spoken – he calls her to watch the TV apology; access is the apology.

### D5. Institutions and press

Club officialdom: genteel absurdity; honors as logistics; the institution misremembers his
peak rank downward and he corrects it – vanity via a single digit. ⭐ The functionary: a
locker-room attendant runs identical scripted encouragement for every player until ONE crisis,
then breaks script once – one script-break per fixture character, maximum value. The agent:
honesty about dishonesty – returns when the player is sellable and says so; represents both
finalists and both lovers and monetizes the conflict cheerfully; yet delivers the one honest
fear monologue. Press, three modes: stat-trap conference · tabloid misattribution engine ·
probing broadcast interview – and the press conference is finally HIJACKED FOR INTIMACY:
institutions can be repurposed by characters, richer than institutions merely opposing.
Umpires as liturgy: fixed formulas form a flat ritual surface against which any outburst
registers loudly.

### D6. Temperament contrast

Peter (steady, fading, low-expressed): hedges, chronic apology (she names the tic),
self-deprecating precision about his own decline, irony under threat; **no direct declaratives
of want until he routes them through an institution** – his love speech happens on television.
Notices logistics, manners, absurdity; omits desire. Lizzie (intense, expressed): leads with
dares and stakes; process questions; doctrine speech; interrupts apologies with threats. She
initiates every private escalation; he initiates the public repair – a clean asymmetry of
arenas. ⭐ Four corners for our grid: Peter closed/steady · Lizzie open/intense · Dieter
open/steady · Jake closed/intense – the cast IS the openness×intensity quadrant.

### D7. Refusals; escalation, interruption, asymmetry

Refusal always protects something NAMED (luck, advantage, ritual, faith). ⭐ **Declarations
arrive embedded and must be excavated** – the first I-love-you is shouted mid-match and
misdelivered; the decisive one is buried inside a scolding and he must ask for the sentence's
other half back. Never let a declaration travel alone. Interruption is the master operator
(schedule, a picked-up phone, press shouting, a blender, a scold, a ring) – comic and dramatic
beats share the cut mechanism. Escalation shapes: stepwise stakes · anaphora · register-break
(politeness → punch → code violation) · public/private inversion (private wound → public
apology). ⭐ **Closure by state-change, not dialogue**: the proposal is never spoken; the father
never apologizes; accounts are settled by attendance, access, results and an epilogue – let
LEDGER changes end arcs that lines opened.
