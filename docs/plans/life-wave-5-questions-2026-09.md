## 1. ⚠⚠ The elite gate's currency DECAYS – and it now refuses every career for the back eight tenths of its life

**His word, 13.09:** «elite gate включим здесь же». It is on (T13).

**Measured (T13, and priced by T10):** `coachHireable` reads `kidPoints(world, 'domestic')`, which
is a **rolling 52-week best-6**, not an achievement. Over 400-week walks, careers cross 150 at weeks
**22–47**, hold it for **19–44** weeks, and then sit below it for **310–349** weeks. **Every one ends
at 0.** The share of careers an Elite coach would refuse, by tenth of career:

| 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th | 7th | 8th | 9th | 10th |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 58.4 % | 74.1 % | **100 %** | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |

The `pro` fixture – week 412, a WTA professional with $4.9M banked – holds **0** domestic points and
would be refused an Elite coach today.

**The question: is «only takes players with results» meant as EVER, or as RIGHT NOW?**

⭐ **Recommendation: a high-water mark.** The sentence is about a career, not about a week, and the
decay is an artefact of a ranking window designed for a different purpose. A gate that opens once
she has proved herself and stays open reads as the market remembering her; the shipped one reads as
the market forgetting her the moment she outgrows the domestic ladder.

## 2. ⚠ «Back on her feet» buys a steady girl almost nothing, and the blur the spec says it dissolved is still there

**The spec §1's claim:** the 23.08 slope-blur is dissolved by the focus model, because the rungs no
longer have to be three speeds of one thing.

**Measured (T10, n=20/cell, paired):** for a **steady** girl the entry rung buys **0.000** weeks and
the whole ladder buys about a quarter of a week – the ladder MISSES its bar three times. For an
**intense** girl it is a clean ladder: 4.7 → 3.7 → 3.2 → 2.8 weeks under the knee, every step above
2×SEM.

It is arithmetic, not tuning: −22 from a lifted 75 at a 5/week return is one or two weeks whatever
the family pays. **So the blur did not dissolve – it moved inside the recovery focus and lives there
for half the roster.**

**The question: re-price `recoverySlope` so a steady girl can feel it, or accept that this focus is
for intense daughters and let the game say so?**

⭐ **Recommendation: accept, and make it legible.** The spec §5 already says every focus has a
natural clientele; this is that, measured. But see question 4 – a card that sells four focuses flat
to a girl two of them cannot help is the same failure in a different coat.

## 3. The cool-head constants are about 1.55× high if the card is meant to be honest

**Measured (T10):** a held season delivers **0.61–0.65×** the number on the card – 0.97 / 1.57 /
2.13 against the ruled 1.5 / 2.5 / 3.5. Decomposed: a held season is **≈38.7 worked weeks, not 52**
(the seat stands down on family and college weeks, ×0.744), and the headroom coupling is ×0.86.
⚠ The 0.9× I predicted came from a probe world with no stand-downs and was wrong.

The ladder itself is sound – each rung beats the one below by 36× and 12× SEM, and the effect at
the ceiling is exactly zero.

**The question: raise the constants so the card's number is what a held year delivers, or leave them
as the ceiling a perfect year would reach?** (O5 made these bench-first, so this is the ruling it
was waiting for.)

## 4. ⚠ A quarter of the roster can buy «Working on herself» and get nothing, and no surface says so

**Measured (T10):** the never-fired corridor for `'herself'` is **100 %** for a **sunny** girl – born
open and steady, so ruling N clamps both axes at 0 and there is nowhere to grow. She can hold the
focus for a career and it cannot move a number.

This is the academy-fares failure (round 23 #16) in a new coat, and it is the one corridor number in
the wave that is not explained by «there was nothing to recover from this week».

**The question: should the card close that focus for a girl who has no growable axis – and if so,
what does it say?**

⚠ The tension, stated plainly: the mechanism already exists (the card closes `'herself'` at a
strained bond with its own sentence), but a «nothing to grow here» state would **tell the player her
temperament**, which this game has never shown on any screen. That is the reason it was not built.

## 5. §2a's «the road back always exists» was arithmetically false, and a cap makes it true

**Measured (T7):** unbounded, 300 grinding weeks reach a lean of −450 and the free walk home takes
about **nine years** – longer than the game. `ECONOMY.life.walls.leanMax = 100` was added as a
PROPOSAL, sized so the deepest hole is ~2 seasons of free repair plus ~60 weeks back to the release
band.

**The question: confirm the cap, or rule a different size.** The promise is yours and it was
unreachable as written.

## 6. §2a's round trip cannot happen under either parent the game ships

**Measured (T10, census v3):** the caring policy produces 0 % walls-raised; the grinding policy
produces 95 % raised and 20 % lowered. **Neither ever changes its mind**, so the round trip – «a
closed-again girl can be opened again», the sentence §2a calls earned drama – is produced only by
the bench's own third arm, which changes policy mid-career and is labelled as a construction.

**The question: is the round trip something the simulated parents should be able to produce, or is
it a sentence the ALBUM writes for a human player who changed how he played?**

## 7. The style fit now matters more than the rung – the numbers, not the comment

**Measured (T12, comment corrected 13.09):** `fitFactor` spans **1.67×** (1.25/0.75) against a
hireable rung ladder of **1.21×** (0.95→1.15). So a **great** fit at the BUDGET rung (1.1875)
out-teaches a **good** fit at the ELITE rung (1.15), and an **off** fit at budget or middle (0.7125 /
0.78) teaches **slower than the parent's own 0.82** while being billed weekly for it.

The stale comment that still described the old balance is fixed. **The question is whether the
numbers are the thing that is wrong.** Round 38 #17 widened this span deliberately; this is the
first measurement of what the widening did to the market's ordering.

## 9. The four focus sentences are on screen for three weeks a year

**Measured (T11):** the note under the focus row is the **engine's refusal** whenever anything is
