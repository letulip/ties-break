# Concept, Plot & Narrative Review – Ties Break: Ace Parent

## Verdict

The core fantasy – you are the parent, not the player or the agent – is not just claimed, it is delivered every single week, through a narrative machine that is technically the most interesting thing in the repo: ~300 lines of licence-gated, honesty-pinned parent-voice copy (diary, travel notes, fridge scraps, memory cards) that cannot contradict the simulation by construction. Pillar 1 (the match as a show) and the tone brief (honest, unglamorous, warm) are shipped and coherent. The two things that matter most now: first, nothing can end – bankruptcy is a stop reason on a loop that keeps ticking, retirement does not exist, and every finale the concept promises is vapor, which means the valley-of-death drama has no terminal stakes (acknowledged and sequenced in the plan, but it is the biggest narrative hole in the product today). Second, pillar 3 has no mechanics – morale, relationship, burnout and the right to quit exist only as prose, and the lore bible overstates the build on exactly this point. Third, fix the first screen: onboarding currently sells the fantasy ('Your kid has real talent… anything is possible') that the entire game exists to argue with.

## Strengths

- **The honesty system is real, not aspirational.** Every diary line carries a `claims` object; selection is licence-filtered; tests sweep the licence space (tests/diary.test.ts, tests/week-notes.test.ts) so a line that could lie about the week is a failing test. The system demonstrably catches real lies – the vacation line about swimming was rewritten because three of six packages have no water (src/engine/diary.ts:2207).
- **The copy is good.** 'She won it, and then asked if we could stop for chips.' 'Second. She is fine. She said so about four times.' 'We talk about money after she goes to bed.' The register is one voice across ~300 lines, with explicit separation rules – parent observes, coach assesses, fridge is domestic-only and vocabulary-swept (src/composables/fridgeNote.ts).
- **Pillar 1 is narrated, not just rendered.** viz/commentary.ts produces 4–6 beats per set with priorities, caps, and deliberate silence; aces and errors are the manner of a beat, never a beat themselves. Skip / key / full modes ship (MatchViewer.vue:113).
- **Drama beats land on paper.** The knock dialog is the app's only no-escape question (KnockDialog.vue:12); the sponsor letter states its own failure mode in the brand's voice and later reports the settled outcome (OfferLetter.vue:27, 110); memories rotate statelessly with anniversaries interrupting.
- **WTA-first is followed through** – gender lock with an honest disabled state, female pools, women-specific peak ages in lore, motherhood research digested for the future adult arc.
- **Narrative bugs get the balance-bug treatment**: the 'she always sleeps after finals' complaint was answered with an instrumented 40-career measurement before any coin was reweighted (diary.ts:519–596).
- **The supposed tone tension is a harmony.** Brutal economics rendered through warm domestic surfaces is the game's signature, and setting.md §3 codifies it: 'Bleak and honest are not the same thing.'

## Findings

**[CRITICAL] No career can end – src/engine/world.ts:4853, docs/specs/adult-tour-and-endings.md §1.** concept-ru.md:33 promises six finals (champion / solid pro / eternal qualifier / bankruptcy / burnout / injury). In the build, `fundsCents < 0` adds a `'funds'` stop reason and the weeks keep ticking; retirement has no code path. The adult rungs and prize cliff (spec items 1–2) HAVE landed (W15/W35/W100 with prizeCents, maxAgeYears:18 on J tiers – season/calendar.ts:128, 281–336), but the fork at 19 and all four endings (items 3–4) have not. A bankrupt family today gets an unending nothing – the exact anticlimax the brand cannot afford. Fix direction: ship spec §4's fork and the epilogue surface next, as already sequenced; until then, at minimum make the `'funds'` stop present itself as a dramatic event rather than a silent stall.

**[HIGH] Pillar 3 has no mechanics – docs/lore/setting.md:88.** Morale, the relationship meter, burnout and the right to quit do not exist in src/engine (fatigue/condition only); the parent's own job/time tradeoff is a flat weekly credit per class. The diary carries the entire 'child is a person' pillar as prose. Worse, setting.md – which opens with 'Everything here is read out of the shipped build' – claims she 'has morale… and the right to quit'. Fix direction: correct the lore bible's §4 now (cheap), and treat the Phase-6 morale/relationship work as the pillar's overdue mechanical half, not as content polish.

**[MEDIUM] Onboarding contradicts the thesis on screen one – OnboardingWizard.vue:331–333.** 'Your kid has real talent. With the right support, anything is possible' asserts what the whole game says nobody knows ('\"Might\" is the whole game' – setting.md:24; «наверняка не знает никто» – concept-ru.md:13), and 'Raise a Champion. Together.' frames her as a project against pillar 3. Fix direction: one register pass – e.g. 'Everyone says she has talent. Nobody knows yet – and the bills start now', keeping paragraph 3, which already has the right voice.

**[MEDIUM] Adjacent-week repetition on the always-visible condition note – diary.ts:1380, 1349–1360.** `diaryLine()` draws free per week; an ordinary quiet week at ok/worn condition licenses exactly two lines, so identical adjacent notes land at p≈1/2 across the training stretches that dominate a season. The codebase documents this exact failure ('Both read as a bug') and fixed it – but only inside `weekNoteFor`'s always-speaking bands (diary.ts:2480–2501). Fix direction: apply the same career-keyed pool-walk to the condition surface (and consider it for the photo surface's quiet band), or widen the two-line subpool.

**[MEDIUM] The lore bible disagrees with itself on the final band – setting.md:178 vs 122–139.** The §5 table records the resolved 27.07 boundaries (adult 23–30, milf 31+, matching `portraitStage()`), while the same section's band header ('29 and up'), its closing brief ('treat its age floor as 29') and §11 still carry the pre-resolution floor; line 139 also still claims decisions.md is unreconciled when decisions.md:152–158 reconciled it on 27.07. Retirement age remains three-valued across plan/decisions/research. Fix direction: one editing pass on §5/§11, and an owner ruling on career end age – §11 itself says nobody can brief the end of the arc until then.

**[LOW] One honesty-pin escape – diary.ts:2086.** 'She had time to be fifteen this week' asserts an age; DiaryFacts has no age field outside birthday weeks and WeekClaims has no age claim, so the pin cannot check it and the line will eventually print about a seventeen-year-old. Fix direction: template on her actual age (the `ageWord` table already exists two screens down) or drop the number.

**[LOW] README is stale in both directions – README.md:28, 5.** 'Status: Concept / planning phase' sits beside a live deployed game 13 playtest rounds in; 'from junior courts to the pro tour' oversells an arc that today ends nowhere. Fix direction: honest status line ('playable junior career, adult tour landing'), which is also better marketing for a game whose brand is honesty.

**[LOW] Start-country choice is cosmetic – decisions.md:22.** The promise 'affects starting conditions (federation strength, costs, calendar distance)' has no engine backing; travel is banded by tier × wealth only. Fix direction: either wire a modest country effect in the economy wave or note the deferral where the decision is recorded, so onboarding's 'Where Are You Starting?' does not imply a difficulty choice.

**[LOW] concept-ru.md drift – concept-ru.md:49–67, 20.** The name-candidate debate still reads as open after the 22.07 decision, and §1 promises between-set tactic correction for the parent-coach setup that has no stub or gate in the build (shouts shipped; coaching did not). Fix direction: a dated postscript, not a rewrite – the doc is otherwise a strong pitch artifact.

## Recommendations

1. **Ship the endings before anything else narrative** (already sequenced as NEXT): the fork at 19, the bankruptcy finale, the injury and decline ends. Every system the diary has built – memories, milestones, the album – is the raw material for the epilogue, and until an ending exists the game's central question ('was it worth it') is unanswerable by design.
2. **Rewrite the three onboarding sentences** to the game's own register. This is the cheapest high-value narrative fix in the repo: the first screen currently makes the reference game's promise.
3. **Correct setting.md** (§4 morale claim, §5/§11 band floor, line 139's stale unreconciled note) – it is the artist-facing contract and it is wrong in three load-bearing places.
4. **Port the pool-walk anti-repeat to `diaryLine`'s condition surface** and add two or three quiet-week condition lines; fix the 'fifteen' line while in the file.
5. **When Phase 6 lands morale/relationship, give the parent a diary presence of their own** – the concept's strongest unused beats are the parent's sacrifices («продать машину ради академии», more shifts = less presence) and no surface ever narrates them; the 'levers change as she grows' arc (decisions.md #19) will need the relationship to be mechanical, not just written.
6. **Update README status and soften 'to the pro tour'** until the adult arc has a voice – the diary deliberately gave it none yet (diary.ts:312–319), which is the right call, and the marketing copy should be equally honest.
