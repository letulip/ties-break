# Game Design & Mechanics Review

## Verdict

The two pillars that are built – the match-as-show and the honest economics – are built to a standard most shipped sims never reach: the Markov math is provably correct, rally rendering is decoupled by architecture, and the economy is calibrated against cited research with Monte-Carlo benches that have falsified their own fixes at least once (rank-plateau.md is a model of how to tune a simulation). The third pillar is the problem: the README sells morale, parent-child relationship and burnout as a present pillar, and none of it exists in the engine – the kid is currently a body plus a narrator, not a psyche. The three actions that matter most: (1) build the morale/relationship layer or stop advertising it, since it is also the declared prerequisite for the quit mechanic and half the promised endings; (2) give careers terminal states – today you can neither win nor lose, bankruptcy is a soft stop and retirement does not exist, which caps the valley-of-death drama the whole game is pitched on; (3) confront the dual-universe tournament design, which double-pays every event she plays and makes it impossible for her to ever take points off a rival – a structural bias against her rank and against rivalry drama that no bench has measured.

## Strengths

- **The match math is right.** I verified the O'Malley hold formula, the exact tiebreak DP (correct n%4 serve rotation, valid gambler's-ruin from any tie state) and the live-probability DP from arbitrary score (`match/closedForm.ts`, `match/liveProb.ts`). Scoring emerges from raw counters, no stored deuce state (`match/scoring.ts`).
- **Pillar 1 holds structurally.** `match/rally.ts` consumes recorded winners on its own per-point RNG stream; aces and double faults are narration of decided points. Rendering cannot influence outcomes.
- **Calibration is empirical.** 10k-mirror-match hold-rate band tests (`tests/match/calibration.test.ts`) pin the engine to research bands; new point-model terms (rally, pace) are difference-shaped so calibrated fixtures stay byte-identical by construction (`match/point.ts`).
- **The economics are genuinely research-anchored.** `docs/research/02-tennis-economics.md` and `junior-economics.md` carry cited figures with data-quality flags; juniors pay to play (real rule); the W15 row in `season/calendar.ts` reproduces the real cliff – winning the tournament roughly pays for the trip; the ITF Appendix F entry cap is modeled by age; the shipped J points are the real ITF table (30/60/300).
- **Balance methodology is the best thing in the repo.** rank-plateau.md ships a predicted fix, measures it doing nothing, and finds the real cause (she plays at condition 19 forever; a rest floor is worth 50 places). knock-rate bench validates the anti-farming claim (pushing doubles injuries/season). Sponsor gates were swept (brand-gate-bench, 216 careers) before numbers were picked. Tuning here is measurement, not vibes.
- **Degeneracies are hunted systematically:** first-round participation income removed (wave B), J30 grinding capped by the real eligibility rule, double-booked rivals fixed with a draw-count-preserving post-pass (`season/tournament.ts resolveDoubleBookings`), a medical veto under the one degenerate bench cell.
- **The ladder is coherent.** Two ranking tables from one ledger (domestic/ITF, plus WTA), on-ramps that open the bottom rung of each table from the table below – the closed-loop problem solved the way reality solves it. The conveyor (`season/conveyor.ts`) gives the field a real pyramid with the age-19 crunch.
- **Difficulty via background is qualitative:** disjoint per-bill wealth corridors, gear service lives the working family must stretch past, a travel-keyed academy rescue, and deliberately background-neutral shoes so money cannot buy out of injury.
- **Where kid-as-person mechanics exist, they are real:** the knock is a genuine two-cost decision with measured negative EV for pushing; the relative age effect is a level-plus-catch-up model whose inverted first version is honestly documented; the fog-of-war radar cannot leak true values by construction.

## Findings

**[HIGH] Pillar 3's psychological core does not exist** – README.md:24 lists morale, relationship, burnout and growth spurts as a present pillar; `grep -rn "morale|burnout|relationship"` across `src/engine` finds nothing mechanical (only diary travel-mood art and a misnamed `trust` variable in coachLoad.ts that is shown stamina). `career-outcome-targets.md` explicitly defers the quit mechanic to \"the morale system (Phase 6)\". Consequence for design: the only cost of a pushy-parent grind is physical (knocks, injuries, condition) – there is no rebellion, no relationship debt, no burnout ending, so the game's most distinctive promise is currently prose. Fix direction: either build a minimal morale/relationship pair wired to the levers that already exist (plan.train, knock pushes, missed vacations, playing hurt) with the quit arc from the targets spec, or re-scope README/concept honestly to \"condition, injury, school\".

**[HIGH] Nothing ends** – `world.ts:4853`: negative funds is a stop reason, not a state; the sim ticks forever; the conveyor retires every rival but cannot touch her. `adult-tour-and-endings.md` names the hole; its ladder half shipped, the endings half did not. Consequence: no run can be lost, so honest-brutal economics has no bite at the exact moment it matters – the targets spec (60-80% survival) is unwireable. Fix direction: implement bankruptcy and age-out endings first (both are pure state machines over data that already exists), the quit ending after morale lands.

**[HIGH] The dual-universe tournament double-pays every event she enters** – `world.ts:4479-4483` runs canonical AI brackets for ALL scheduled events including hers; `runAiTournament` (world.ts:3859) ledgers full finishes (a champion's full points), then `finalizeTournament` adds her shadow-run points on top. She can never remove points from a rival; the rival she beats in a final banks whatever the canonical universe gave him the same week. This is a deliberate trade for RNG identity (\"the canonical AI world stays the same world whatever she does\"), but it is a standing thumb on the scale against her rank – rank-plateau.md measured her 5 raw-power points above her point-peers and attributed the plateau to fatigue without ever quantifying this term – and it quietly hollows the rivalry system (rival.ts makes rivals tired; it cannot make them beatable in the standings). Fix direction: at minimum, bench the bias (re-run the rank-plateau harness with the kid's event excluded from canonical payout); if material, pay the entered event's AI results out of the shadow bracket instead – it is the same draw-count discipline, one universe fewer.

**[MEDIUM] Two entry rule systems for the same draws** – kid: absolute point bands / acceptance cuts; AI: percentile ambition windows plus backfills that reach outside the band (`season/tournament.ts:197-233`). rank-plateau.md lists it \"still open\"; the split was replicated onto the W rungs. Fix direction: unify on the acceptance-cut reading (the two-ladders work already moved the kid's gates to shares of the table); make the AI enter through the same cut.

**[MEDIUM] Five radar axes, five very different weights** – serve/ret at 0.0016 p/point on every point vs composure's break-point-only max 0.03 (≈1-2% of serve's leverage) and stamina's past-point-120 capped term (`match/point.ts:52-61`). Research-faithful (Klaassen-Magnus effects ARE small) and not a trap (training is not per-axis), but the radar visually implies parity, and coach-fit trades are made across attributes of unequal worth. Fix direction: either widen composure's remit (it is the natural carrier for a future morale coupling) or let the coach's notes say which axes decide matches.

**[MEDIUM] Calibration authority is drifting owner-ward** – `economy.ts:84-99` records wealthy income raised 430→750 on the owner's third ask and middle 300→425 on the second, each time re-pinning the calibration test to the new measured window. The corridor header also promises a revisit \"when real incomes (prize money) land\" – prize money has landed (TierDef.prizeCents) and the revisit has not. Individually defensible; as a pattern it converts the economy suite from constraint to recorder. Fix direction: re-run the econ bench against the career-outcome targets (the spec written precisely for this) and re-derive the bands once, top-down.

**[LOW] Stale header math in the tier catalogue** – `season/calendar.ts:110-112` still says j60 = ×1.5, j300 = ×2.5 and \"j30 already out-scores a National at every finish\"; the shipped tables are ×2 / ×10 and National out-scores j30 at every finish. A load-bearing comment an owner will tune from. Fix: rewrite the comment.

**[LOW] Fault-side serve stats are skill-blind** – `match/rally.ts:26-30`: DF rate flat 9%, first-serve-in flat 62% for everybody, while the ace rate now scales with modeled serve speed. Box scores tell players apart on aces but not on DF%. Fix direction: scale DF on composure/serve the same post-draw way aces were done.

**[LOW] Injuries are dodgeable with foresight** – fixed per-week roll vs choice-dependent threshold (`world.ts:1829-1885`): a reloading player can duck any seen injury by lowering tau (vacation ×0.25, skip the event, physio). The cost of the otherwise excellent post-draw-multiply discipline. Fix direction: accept it (single-save UX makes it niche) or fold the week's tau inputs into the roll's key.

**[LOW] Displayed odds ignore the modifiers the match runs with** – `liveProb.ts` composes base p only; the kid's matches simulate with momentum/big-point/fatigue. Small systematic drift in a project with a preview-odds-honesty spec. Fix direction: state the approximation in the spec or bench the gap once.

## Recommendations

1. **Build or retract pillar 3.** A minimal morale + relationship pair wired to existing levers (train slider, knock pushes, playing hurt, missed holidays), then the quit arc from career-outcome-targets.md. Until then, soften README/concept claims – the game currently over-promises its most differentiating feature.
2. **Ship endings.** Bankruptcy and age-out first (pure state over existing data), quit after morale. Wire the outcome targets as bench gates the same week.
3. **Measure the dual-universe rank bias** with the rank-plateau harness; if material, pay the entered event's AI results from the shadow bracket. This also makes rivalry real, which pillar 1's drama needs.
4. **Unify kid/AI entry rules** on the acceptance-cut model before more rungs are added.
5. **One top-down economy re-derivation** against the outcome targets now that prize money exists, instead of further per-knob owner asks.
6. Housekeeping: fix the stale calendar.ts header math; scale DF% on skill; document the odds approximation.
