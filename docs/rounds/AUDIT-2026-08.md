## What would stop this happening again

The three real failures here were not coding failures. **A branch is not a tick** – round 15 lost
thirteen boxes to four branches that landed hours later. **An answer written in another file is not
an answer** – R8-3's verdict has existed since 09.08 in a triage nobody re-read. **A question inside
a round is work** – six of the nine dropped items are questions that were asked, priced, and then
simply stopped being mentioned. `README.md` § "Keeping this true" already names all three, and says
in its own last paragraph that nothing enforces it. That is still true: the fix is a mechanical
check at merge time, not a fourth audit.
